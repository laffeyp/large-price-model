## Why v0.3 exists

Sprint 020 wired the Alpha-Vantage fetcher into the probe CLI and ran a live smoke against real vendor endpoints. The fetcher raised `AlphaVantageResponseError` on the VIX channel — `TIME_SERIES_INTRADAY` does not accept `^VIX` (VIX is an index, not an intraday-priced security). The v0.2 vocabulary offered no signal shape for "the fetcher raised an exception on this sample date." Every field of `CHANNEL_PROBED` is a strict enum or a typed timestamp; the vocabulary correctly refused each attempt to serialise a non-observation.

Sprint 020 responded by writing a `_error_shim` in `scripts/probe_channels.py` that caught the exception and returned a `FetchResult` with fabricated field values: `actual_frequency="15min"`, `earliest_timestamp="1970-01-01T00:00:00+00:00"`, `revision_behavior="immutable"`, `missing_fraction=1.0`. Seven fabricated fields per failed date, five failed dates, thirty-five fabricated field-writes in the trace. The vocabulary was doing its job; the code was lying to work around it. The Architect flagged this on 2026-08-11 as a code failure, not a vocabulary gap.

The correct sequence, missed at Sprint 020: halt-and-articulate at the first emit-time refusal, evolve the vocabulary if truly insufficient, then wire the honest code. Sprint 022 executes that sequence for real.

## What changed

### One new tag

`CHANNEL_FETCH_FAILED` (category `probe`, stratum `incident`) fires when a fetcher raises during a probe attempt. Six required payload fields: `channel`, `symbol`, `source`, `sample_date`, `exception_class`, `error_message`. No fabricated observation — the tag records the fact of a failed attempt without pretending to have data. The channel-level `CHANNEL_COVERAGE_ASSESSED` still closes with whatever aggregate the successful probes produce.

Design distinction from `CHANNEL_REJECTED` (which stays unchanged): `CHANNEL_REJECTED` means "we got data but it fails coverage thresholds"; `CHANNEL_FETCH_FAILED` means "we got no data at all — the vendor call itself broke." Two different runtime events; two different signal shapes.

### One new evidence constraint

`diagnostic_required` on `CHANNEL_FETCH_FAILED.error_message`: non-empty string, captures the vendor error verbatim. A Reviewer reading the trace sees exactly why the fetch failed. Matches the incident-stratum halt condition every other incident tag satisfies.

### What did NOT change

- 55 → 56 tags. Every prior tag unchanged in payload and stratum.
- 14 categories, 4 session strata, 24 operators unchanged.
- Grammar-growth `project_overrides` unchanged (the addition follows the established `struct` and Layer-2 patterns; no new project override needed).

### What still needs to change but did NOT in v0.3

The vocabulary still requires `earliest_timestamp: datetime_utc` on both `CHANNEL_REJECTED` and `CHANNEL_COVERAGE_ASSESSED`. When zero fetches succeed for a channel, there is no earliest_timestamp — and honestly serialising that state requires either an optional-field evolution or a policy of halting the run on all-failed channels. Sprint 023 will choose halt (raise a `NoObservationsError` when a channel has zero successful probes; the CLI exits nonzero). A future v0.4 could mark the timestamp fields optional if the operational reality demands survey-continue behavior; deferred until a real sprint needs it.
